IQOS · Philip MorrisLifecycle designBehavior triggersRetention

Challenge

Customers moved through onboarding at different speeds. Some acted quickly. Others needed more support. All of them interacted through more than one channel: the website, email, SMS and the customer-service center.

A single scheduled sequence could not reflect that. A website action or a service call could make the next planned message irrelevant. The business needed one connected lifecycle, not separate channel campaigns.

What I designed

I mapped a six-month lifecycle around customer engagement and product adoption, rather than a fixed communication schedule. The Product Adoption Assessment acts as a structured CRM signal. Its outcome is recorded in the customer profile and determines the next communication, support action or lifecycle path.

The engagement check draws on email and SMS activity, website behavior, purchases, service interactions and CRM status, not on any one signal alone. Every meaningful website or customer-service interaction can change the customer's next digital step.

How the journey adapts to customer signals

Completed the conversion milestone. The journey shifts to adoption, loyalty and relevant next actions.

My role

  • Mapped the six-month onboarding and conversion lifecycle
  • Defined engagement checks, conversion statuses and customer milestones
  • Designed personalized triggers, journey branches and suppression rules
  • Connected website behavior and service-center outcomes with the digital journey
  • Directed Salesforce Marketing Cloud and Salesforce CRM implementation with vendor and global technology teams

I defined the business, customer and CRM logic in coordination with local stakeholders and global technology teams responsible for technical implementation.

Result

+15%conversion rate vs. the previous onboarding journey
~20,000customers through the journey, Dec 2025–present
IQOS · Philip MorrisIntent scoringLead nurturingConversion strategy

Challenge

About 50,000 existing adult leads, collected through field activity, included consumers known to use competing cigarette brands. The business needed a way to identify genuine intent before investing in conversion messaging.

What I designed

Consumers completed a value-exchange engagement activity built around a short set of questions. Responses fed an intent-scoring model that classified each consumer as low, mid or high intent.

Mid and high intent consumers entered a multichannel nurturing journey coordinated between CRM and the customer-service center: education on the product category, a one-click callback request, and a relevant offer introduced later in the journey.

Field leads to conversion decision

My role

  • Activated an existing competitor-lead database through a structured engagement journey
  • Designed the intent-scoring approach and low, mid and high audience paths
  • Structured the multichannel nurturing journey across CRM and customer service
  • Defined the educational sequence, callback flow, offer timing and eligibility rules
  • Owned post-launch performance analysis and used cross-channel insights to refine targeting, messaging, timing and subsequent waves
  • Planned a phased three-wave rollout with call-center readiness checks and UAT sign-off ahead of each wave

Result

14%of the 50,000 leads scored mid/high intent and entered the nurture journey
<1%opt-out rate across the journey
